# InvoiceNow — Privacy Policy

**By DevIgnite Studios**

**Effective date:** 13 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to InvoiceNow, an invoice and quotation management application developed by DevIgnite Studios.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you use InvoiceNow.

This Privacy Policy explains the information processed by the application, how it is stored, and the choices available to you.

## 2. Information You Enter

InvoiceNow allows you to enter and manage information including:

- Business names, addresses, registration numbers, email addresses and phone numbers.
- Customer names, company details and contact information.
- Invoice and quotation details, including items, quantities, prices, taxes and payment instructions.
- Business logos and document preferences.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the appropriate authority to enter and use personal information belonging to your customers.

## 3. Local Data Storage

InvoiceNow is designed to store invoice, quotation, customer and business profile information locally on your device.

DevIgnite Studios does not operate a dedicated server for storing your invoice or customer records.

Information you enter into the application is not automatically sent to DevIgnite Studios.

## 4. Backup and Restore

InvoiceNow provides backup and restore functionality.

You can export backup files and choose where to save them, including compatible storage locations available through Apple's Files interface.

If you choose iCloud Drive or another cloud storage provider, your backup files may be stored and processed according to that provider's privacy policies.

Daily and weekly backup preferences may prepare backups when the application is opened. These preferences do not guarantee automatic uploads to cloud storage.

You are responsible for keeping exported backup files secure.

## 5. PDF Generation and Sharing

InvoiceNow allows you to generate and share PDF invoices and quotations.

PDF documents may contain business, customer and transaction information that you entered.

When you share a PDF through another application or service, that service may process the shared information under its own privacy policy.

## 6. Subscriptions and Payments

InvoiceNow offers an optional InvoiceNow Pro auto-renewable subscription.

Subscriptions and payments are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit services.

DevIgnite Studios does not directly collect or store your full payment card information through InvoiceNow.

Apple may process purchase, subscription and transaction information according to Apple's privacy policies.

## 7. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

InvoiceNow does not intentionally include third-party advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

The application does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Apple may independently collect diagnostic or App Store information according to your device settings and Apple's policies.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your locally stored business records remain on your device until you delete them through the application's available features or remove the application's local data.

Deleting the application may remove locally stored records, subject to device backups and Apple's data management behavior.

Previously exported backup files are not automatically deleted when you remove the application. You must manage those files separately.

## 9. Security

InvoiceNow uses native Apple platform technologies for local data management and document handling.

No application or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

We recommend protecting your device with a passcode or biometric authentication and storing exported backup files securely.

## 10. Children's Privacy

InvoiceNow is intended for business and professional use and is not designed specifically for children.

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated account or server.

## 11. International Availability

InvoiceNow may be available to users in Singapore and other countries.

When you use external services such as iCloud Drive, Apple's App Store or third-party sharing applications, information may be processed in other jurisdictions under those providers' terms and policies.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the application, its features or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
